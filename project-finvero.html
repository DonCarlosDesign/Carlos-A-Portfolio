Concept Project

Finvero—Brand Identity for an AI Finance App

A brand identity concept for a seed-stage fintech startup building an AI-driven budgeting and investing app for Gen Z and Millennials.

Brand Identity Logo Design Fintech Startup

Client

Finvero (concept)

Role

Brand Identity Designer

Industry

Fintech Startup

Year

2025

01

The Brief

Finvero needed a brand identity that stood apart from the "cold and corporate" fintech norm, something modern and friendly enough to earn trust from a young, digital-first user base before the app even launched.

02

The Approach

Built the identity around a simple flowing "F" mark in a fresh teal-to-navy gradient—approachable enough for social media, credible enough for a banking app. Paired it with a conversational, empowering brand voice instead of typical finance-speak.

03

The Direction

Delivered a horizontal wordmark lockup, a standalone app icon, and a dark-navy splash screen treatment, a starting system built to extend cleanly into stationery, social templates, and a future UI kit.

Deliverables

Scoped as a concept brand package, not a full production build.

  • Primary logo lockup (horizontal) and standalone app icon
  • Color palette: teal-to-navy gradient with supporting neutrals
  • Business card layout and app splash screen treatment
  • Brand voice direction: friendly, empowering, tech-savvy
