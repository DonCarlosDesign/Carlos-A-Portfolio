Concept Project

Vantrex Global Holdings: 2024 Annual Report

An editorial design concept for a multinational logistics, energy, and infrastructure holding company's fiscal year 2024 annual report.

Editorial Design Data Visualization Corporate

Client

Vantrex Global Holdings (concept)

Role

Editorial & Data Visualization Designer

Industry

Logistics, Energy & Infrastructure

Year

2025

01

The Brief

Vantrex operates across 12 countries and needed an annual report that made a complex, diversified portfolio legible. One that built stakeholder trust through clarity, not just polish.

02

The Approach

Took cues from reports by Deloitte, McKinsey, and Tesla: corporate blues and grays with a gold accent, strong visual hierarchy, and data visualization treated as the lead storytelling device rather than an afterthought.

03

The Direction

Designed a cover, financial performance spread, global impact map, and operational excellence page, each built to hold its own as a standalone slide while reading as one consistent system across the full report.

Deliverables

Scoped as a concept editorial layout, not the full report build.

Cover and section-opener layout system
Financial performance spread with key metric callouts
Global impact map with regional data points
Operational excellence page with donut and bar chart treatments
